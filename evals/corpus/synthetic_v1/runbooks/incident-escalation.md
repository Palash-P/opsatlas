# Incident Escalation Process

Synthetic operational reference for OpsAtlas retrieval evaluation. This is not a real production process.

## Severity levels
- SEV-1: customer-facing outage or confirmed data loss.
- SEV-2: degraded service with a workaround available.
- SEV-3: limited impact or a single internal user affected.

## Escalation path
1. Page the service on-call engineer for SEV-1 and SEV-2 incidents.
2. Escalate to the incident commander when more than one team is involved.
3. Notify stakeholders through the incident channel.
4. Open a postmortem for every SEV-1 incident.

## Communication
- Post an initial status update within fifteen minutes.
- Record decisions and timestamps in the incident timeline.
- Do not speculate about root cause before evidence is available.

## Important distinction
Escalating an incident is not the same as declaring a root cause.

This document is synthetic and is not an approved production process.
